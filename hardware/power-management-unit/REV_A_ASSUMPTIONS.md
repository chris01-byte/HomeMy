## Review disposition

The configurable deviations concern provisional values, not authorization to alter locked topology. All unknown manufacturer guarantees remain explicitly unknown: main hot resistance at9.2 V, full installed-capacitance hot SOA, shared-sense failure independence, capacitor effective/ripple sharing and repetitive resistor duty need review or evidence appropriate to the intended test. A bounded measurement fixture can proceed only within its reviewed envelope; its success does not authorize the whole battery system.

No real hardware order, assembly, battery connection or actuator test is performed by these documents. CAD completion and its own ERC/DRC reports are separate deliverables.
