Bourns® Model CSS and CSM Shunt Series Fixed Resistors
Additional Supplier for Electron Beam Welding Process
To support increasing demand, secure continuity of supply and provide maximum flexibility to our 
customers, Bourns will begin using an additional supplier for the Electron Beam Welding (EBW) process 
for the below listed Model Series. EBW is used in the manufacturing of raw materials for the shunt model 
series listed below. The resistive material used will remain the same and will be carried over from the 
current manufacturer.  Copper materials used in the production of the products will remain under the same 
specifications. The manufacturing process at Bourns will also remain the same and the additional EBW 
supplier is undergoing AEC-Q200 evaluation. 
There is no change to the form, fit, function, quality or reliability of the products.
