The exact ring lugs, qualified crimp tools, cable insulation, ferrules, strain relief and heat-spreader attachment remain assembly BOM selections. The press-fit terminal requires a qualified board-hole/plating/insertion process. These open items must remain visible in the order review; neither clean CAD checks nor a general supplier name substitutes for their evidence.
