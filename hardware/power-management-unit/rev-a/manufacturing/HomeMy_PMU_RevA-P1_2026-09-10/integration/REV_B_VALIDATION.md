# PMU Revision B validation register

`rev_a_engineering_prototype: true`  
`rev_b_production: false`  
Date: 2026-09-10. All measurement gates below are **open**. The design, source-data calculations and any later CAD reports are not physical validation results.

Revision A supplies the measurement platform. Missing post-build data does not by itself stop schematic/layout work. Before energizing any fixture, close its applicable [design-review and energization gates](DESIGN_REVIEW_AND_BRINGUP.md). Unresolved manufacturer proof gaps must receive an explicit engineering disposition for that fixture; they cannot be called measurements that merely await paperwork.

Every record must identify PCB/BOM revision, fitted options, firmware/Linux versions, calibration, instruments, source/current/energy limits, ambient and initial temperatures, harness geometry, actual load, raw data, acceptance criterion, result and reviewer. Preserve failed results and the change needed to repeat the test. No actuator motion is authorized by this register.

## Closing a gate

Close a row only when its raw evidence, reproducible analysis and accepted resulting design/settings are linked. If a measurement changes a part, threshold, connector or layout, update the calculations and repeat affected lower-level tests. A temperature/scope trace that exceeded a limit remains a failed case even if a later revision passes.

Production release requires all rows applicable to the final system, the nine production-gate categories in `requirements.yaml`, finalized harness/mechanics and accepted fault behavior. Revision A remains an engineering prototype until that separate release record exists.
